The objective

Tradition meets modern UX

The Jefferson's Bourbon website redesign aimed to blend tradition with innovation, reflecting the brand's legacy while embracing modern design. The goal was a sleek, user-friendly platform that captivates visitors and enhances their bourbon buying journey — integrating best-in-class UX principles with AI-accelerated research and validation.

Business goals

  • Improve discoverability of limited and essential bourbon collections
  • Increase "Where to Buy" interactions and on-site buying experience
  • Drive higher mobile engagement

User goals

  • Quickly understand the difference between bottles
  • Find flavor notes, awards, and popularity at a glance
  • Easily locate availability online or nearby
  • Get assistance choosing the perfect bottle for self or gifting

About the brand

Jefferson's Bourbon

A premium spirit brand with strong storytelling around craft and experimentation. Founded in 1997 by Trey Zoeller and his father Chet, a famed bourbon historian, Jefferson's continues a family whiskey tradition dating to Trey's 8th-generation grandmother — arrested in 1799 for "production and sales of spirituous liquors." The brand pushes bourbon boundaries while upholding tradition.

Problem statement

Premium spirits, high-friction discovery

Premium spirits customers struggle to compare variants, understand flavor profiles, and find where to buy without friction. The initial site relied heavily on visual blocking but didn't provide a clear product exploration journey or conversion cues — forcing users to rely on acquired knowledge to select and buy.

Key opportunities

01

Guide product selection via an intelligent Bourbon Finder recommender

02

Improve conversion with store locator, where to buy, and on-site buying

03

Elevate brand story with purposeful, scannable content modules

AI-driven process

How AI accelerated every phase

This project applied an AI-led design workflow — human judgment at the center, AI handling synthesis, variation, and validation at scale. Below is the high-level process that shaped the redesign.

Phase 01

Discover & synthesize

AI clustered heuristic audit findings across Nielsen's 10 heuristics and competitive benchmarks (Woodford Reserve, Buffalo Trace, Blanton's, Maker's Mark). Interview transcripts from 6 guerrilla sessions were synthesized into thematic insights and proto-personas.

  • Automated competitive pattern analysis
  • Research synthesis & affinity mapping
  • Proto-persona generation (3 segments)

Outcome: Problem space defined in days, not weeks — 3 validated personas and 12 prioritized UX opportunities.

Phase 02

Generate & diverge

AI-assisted ideation produced dozens of IA and interaction concepts — Bourbon Finder flows, comparison patterns, flavor badge systems, and mobile-first navigation structures. I curated and stress-tested against persona scenarios.

  • Concept variation for Finder & Compare flows
  • Information architecture options
  • Task flow generation for 3 primary scenarios

Outcome: 4 divergent directions narrowed to 1 validated IA — Products, Finder, Story, Where to Buy.

Phase 03

Prototype & validate

Hi-fidelity responsive prototypes were tested in moderated sessions. AI helped generate test scripts, scenario tasks, and projected impact models based on usability benchmarks and industry CPG data.

  • Rapid wireframe → hi-fi iteration
  • Usability test script generation
  • Projected metrics modeling

Outcome: 92% task success on bottle discovery; 95% on expression comparison.

Phase 04

Deliver & iterate

Final deliverables included a modular design system, accessibility specs, and a validation roadmap. AI documented component states, flavor badge taxonomy, and A/B test hypotheses for post-launch measurement.

  • Design system component documentation
  • Accessibility audit checklists
  • Post-launch validation plan

Outcome: Production-ready UI kit with 18 components and clear next-step experiments.

Research insights

What users told us

The Curious Newbie

Wants clarity without expert pressure. Needs a guided Finder, flavor cues in 5 seconds, and a prominent Where to Buy.

The Bourbon Enthusiast

Seeks side-by-side comparisons, aging details (ocean aging), awards, and limited release visibility.

The Occasion Gifter

Values packaging, price clarity, and confidence. Needs occasion-based Finder and instant retailer access.

"On mobile I just want 3 things: products, flavor info, where to buy." — Guerrilla interview participant
Solution

Key design decisions

Bourbon Finder

3–5 step guided quiz — taste preference, proof tolerance, occasion, budget — outputting 1–3 recommended bottles with reasons and buy links.

Product comparison

Side-by-side cards with flavor badges (oak, vanilla, caramel, spice), proof, aging method, awards, and price band.

Enhanced product pages

Above-the-fold tasting notes, sticky Compare and Where to Buy actions, scannable Story / Process / Awards tabs.

Where to Buy

Auto-detect location, map + list view, retailer details, and buy-online options with clear feedback states.

Scroll storytelling

Chaptered brand narrative — Heritage → Experimentation → Ocean Aging → Craft — with reduced-motion support.

Mobile-first IA

Products · Finder · Story · Where to Buy · Journal — with persistent Finder and buy CTAs.

Reflection

Balancing craft narrative with decision simplicity

This redesign proves that AI-led UX doesn't mean sacrificing brand soul. By foregrounding a Bourbon Finder, elevating comparison, and clarifying buying paths, the experience becomes more educational, faster, and more conversion-friendly — without losing the craft narrative that makes Jefferson's special. AI accelerated research synthesis and validation; human judgment shaped every interaction.
