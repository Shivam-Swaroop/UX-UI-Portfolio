AI-led workflow

How I integrate AI into every design phase

A repeatable framework I use with product and engineering teams — human judgment stays in control, AI handles the heavy lifting.

01

Discover & synthesize

AI aggregates interviews, support tickets, and analytics into thematic clusters. I validate patterns and define the problem space with stakeholders.

  • Research synthesis agents
  • Affinity mapping automation
  • Opportunity scoring
02

Generate & diverge

Prompt-driven ideation produces dozens of concepts in hours. I curate, combine, and stress-test against user mental models and business constraints.

  • Concept variation engines
  • Design system-aware generation
  • Competitive pattern analysis
03

Prototype & validate

AI-assisted prototyping accelerates fidelity. Synthetic users and moderated tests reveal friction early — before engineering commits to build.

  • Rapid interactive prototypes
  • AI-moderated usability tests
  • Accessibility pre-checks
04

Ship & iterate

Post-launch, AI monitors behavioral signals and surfaces anomalies. I translate data into design iterations and feed learnings back into the system.

  • Behavioral anomaly detection
  • Feedback loop automation
  • Design debt tracking

Human-in-the-loop, always

AI proposes; designers and users dispose. No automated decision ships without explicit review.

Transparency by default

Every AI touchpoint is visible to users. Confidence levels, sources, and fallbacks are part of the interface.

Measure what matters

Speed gains mean nothing without quality. I track task success, trust, and accessibility alongside velocity.
