My story

Twelve years of saying
"let's find out."

I came to UX through the back door — a graphic design degree that made me obsessed with how things look, followed by a user interview at my first agency that taught me how much more I cared about how things work.

That tension — between craft and function, between what's beautiful and what's usable — has driven every role I've taken since. I've worked in agencies and in-house, with early-stage startups and FTSE 100s, with teams of two and teams of forty.

What I've learned is that the problems are almost always the same: the team is shipping too fast to understand who they're shipping for, or they understand the user but can't get stakeholders to act on it, or they have great research but no process to translate it into decisions.

My job is to build the conditions where good design can actually happen — the culture, the process, the vocabulary, and the trust. Then get out of the way so the designers can do their best work.

Since 2022 I've been embedding AI-assisted workflows into every phase of the design process — not as a novelty, but as infrastructure. Synthesis agents, generative ideation, AI-moderated testing, post-launch anomaly detection. Human judgment stays in control. AI handles the heavy lifting.

"Great design culture is built on psychological safety, clear critique frameworks, and shared ownership of outcomes."

Research before assumptions

I don't let internal opinions substitute for user evidence. Every significant design decision traces back to something a real person said, did, or struggled with.

Transparency over theatre

Design presentations should inform decisions, not perform effort. I show process honestly — including the dead ends and the things that surprised us.

Outcomes over outputs

The measure of a good design isn't the number of screens shipped. It's whether the thing we shipped changed user behaviour in the direction we intended.

Craft as a signal

Attention to visual and interaction detail isn't vanity — it's a signal to every collaborator that this team takes the user experience seriously. Quality is contagious.
